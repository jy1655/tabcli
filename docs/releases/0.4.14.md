# Terminal Agent Bridge 0.4.14

0.4.14 adds the third part of #216: `close-session SESSION --explicit --with-started-from`
closes, after SESSION, each open session whose recorded origin is SESSION, one level deep,
through that session's own ordinary close (#219, #233). Without the flag, `close-session` is
unchanged. No record schema changed; records written by 0.4.x are read as before.

## Changes

- `close-session --with-started-from` (#219). After the named session is closed and the
  started-from scan has listed the open sessions whose origin it is, the command closes each
  listed session in order, within the one `--timeout-secs` budget, through the same close
  procedure its own `close-session` would run: dead-owner repair, owner attestation and the
  terminal close. The scan keeps bounded evidence of every candidate (its status and
  generation, the launch Claim, the initial receipt and its source, the current Claim), and
  the command re-verifies the records immediately before each close; a session that closed,
  changed or disappeared since the scan is skipped and reported with the reason
  (`closed_since_scan`, `close_in_progress`, `session_changed: …`, `source_changed`,
  `session_gone`, the origin-observation reasons for an invalid source). Expiry of the shared
  budget is a failure for the remaining targets. Each closed target's own started-from
  sessions are reported under its entry and never closed. The JSON result adds `closed`,
  `skipped` and `failed` under `started_from`; `ok` is false, and the exit non-zero, when any
  target failed, after the complete report. The origin link grants no authority: a target
  whose owner attestation cannot decide, or whose launch is still pending, is refused exactly
  as its own close would be. Sessions started after the scan are out of scope. Documented in
  [docs/cli.md](../cli.md).

## Why

On 2026-10-16 a Codex session launched for a plan critique ran its memory consolidation and
delegated the Phase 2 audit through `tabcli ask` with provider pi and the repository as the
workspace, which the workspace-keyed admission of #217 does not refuse; the child stayed open
until it was found by hand the next day (#216, comment of 2026-10-17). Its origin was recorded
exactly; this flag lets an explicit close of the parent close such a child too.
