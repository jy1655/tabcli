# Terminal Agent Bridge 0.4.6

0.4.6 moves three more pieces of knowledge behind the seams that own them and changes no output.
Each adapter declares the facts that consent, the binding wait and the terminal seam used to
branch on by name; every macOS terminal adapter owns its complete operation set behind one lookup;
and the `--json` envelope has one home. Records written by 0.4.x are read as before.

## Changes

- Consent, binding and the terminal seam take declared facts. Every provider adapter declares its
  launch-trust facts (whether it answers a workspace dialog at launch, whether its bypass suppresses
  the dialog, and its applied marker), the three macOS terminal adapters declare their binding wait
  (host name, cancellation message, budget policy), and five Windows helpers of the terminal seam
  became private. `complete_launch` and the native-trust wait are neutral over the declarations with
  the same observation order and strings as before.
- Each macOS adapter owns its complete operation set. The dispatcher is one lookup by kind over
  `MacTerminalAdapter`; iTerm2 and Terminal.app hold their own screen scripts and presence probes,
  the response vocabulary helpers live beside the AppleScript runner, and the dead WindowsConsole
  and unreachable arms are gone. Scripts, strings and the open flow's order are unchanged; a
  WindowsConsole handle that reaches a macOS read, guarded-input or presence path now gets the
  lookup's single message.
- The `--json` envelope has one home. `envelope` owns the shared defaults, the conditional
  parse-failure emission and the pretty output; every command keeps its fields, its override order,
  its plain text and its error propagation. A byte-for-byte golden comparison over a frozen state root
  (292 read-only commands and parse failures) matched the previous release.
